# Privacy

ALO knows your day. We don't.

## What ALO observes

- The app in front and how long it stays there
- The project you are working in, when the app makes that clear (a repository, a workspace, a document title)
- Structured events from connected tools: a commit, a pull request, a meeting, a document edit

## What ALO never captures

- Screenshots or screen recordings
- Keystrokes
- Message bodies, document text or file contents
- Passwords, clipboard contents or full URLs

## What stays on your device

Everything ALO records: events, sessions, memories, replays and settings. History is stored in a local database with retention you choose, and you can delete a day or everything at any time.

## What can leave your device

| What | When |
| --- | --- |
| Encrypted sync bundles | Between your own paired devices, if you turn sync on |
| Compressed context | Only when you use an AI feature, and only to the provider you choose |
| Anonymous update check | When the app checks for a new version |

Before anything is sent for AI processing, ALO shows the exact context and its size in bytes.

## Sync

Each device creates its own identity keys. Pairing uses a QR code and a short safety code you can compare. History moves between devices as signed, encrypted bundles. If a relay is used, it only forwards ciphertext.

## Private Zones

Private Zones stop ALO before anything is written: specific apps, domains or projects, a private mode for the next hour, or a pause. A blocked moment leaves no event and no breadcrumb.

## AI providers

You decide whether AI is used at all.

| Option | Where processing happens |
| --- | --- |
| None | Nowhere. ALO works without AI. |
| Local model | On your device |
| Your own key | With the provider you configure |
| ALO AI | ALO's service, with compressed context only |
